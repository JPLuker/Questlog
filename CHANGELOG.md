# Changelog

## 0.5.0
- **Share into Quest Log:** share text from any app (email, chat, a ticket page) and it opens as a new quest to confirm. The subject or first line becomes the title; the rest goes in the notes.
- **Weekly summary:** WEEK in the Log shows what you finished this week (or earlier weeks) by day, with a COPY button for a plain-text list.
- **Templates:** open a quest and tap TEMPLATE to save it; the ★ button next to + adds it again in one tap, with its checklist reset. No schedules and nothing missed.
- **Checklists:** add steps inside a quest and tick them off. The list shows STEPS 2/5. The quest is still finished and rated as one.
- **Search the Log** by title, notes or checklist steps.
- **Cosmetic loot:** finishing a quest sometimes drops a cape, hat or weapon look (45 in all; your first quest always drops one). Wear them from the popup or in Edit Hero. Looks only, never XP. The same quest always rolls the same way, so undo and redo can't farm loot; undoing a quest takes its drop back.
- **Badges:** 22 milestones on the Hero tab (quests finished, quests rated 5, levels, XP in a day, days active, loot), with a toast when you earn one.
- **Sound and vibration** on finishing a quest, levelling up, loot and badges. Both can be turned off in the ☰ menu.
- Closing a quest by tapping outside it or pressing back now keeps your edits.

## 0.4.0
- Title screen on first launch: the app's name, your hero at three gear stages, three lines on how it works, and START.
- The tutorial is now a 12-step guided tour with a step counter. It runs on your first quest (add it, open it and add notes, tap the box, rate it, see the XP bar), then shows the Hero tab and gear, the Log and Undo, and the menu, and ends with "no due dates, no penalties". Skip any time; replay from the ☰ menu.

## 0.3.0
- Notes on quests: tap a quest to open it and write notes (several lines, up to 4,000 characters). The first line shows under the quest in the list and the log.
- Finished quests in the log open the same way, so notes stay readable after you're done.

## 0.2.0
- Hero creator on first launch: body (6), skin tone (5), hair (14 or none) and a name. Change it any time from the Hero tab or the ☰ menu.
- Tutorial on your first quest: add it, tap the box, rate it, see the XP bar. Skip any time; replay it from the ☰ menu.
- The hero is now drawn from layered parts, so gear sits on whatever hero you made.
- Saves and backups from 0.1.x keep working: they skip the tutorial and ask you to make a hero once.

## 0.1.1
- Test builds: every push to main makes a "Quest Log TEST" APK (Actions artifact) that installs beside the real app. It uses a throwaway key, so a newer test build needs the old one uninstalled first; export a backup before doing that.
- Pushes to main no longer fail while the signing key isn't set up yet; tags still require it.

## 0.1.0
- Quests: type one and press Enter, tap the box when it's done, rate it 1–5 (green to red). The rating is the XP.
- Hero: levels from XP (each level needs 5 more XP than the last), nine gear looks from LV 1 to LV 30, stats for total XP, quests done, today and best day.
- Log: finished quests by day with their XP; Undo puts a quest back and takes its XP back.
- Tap a quest to edit or delete it.
- Backup export and import from the ☰ menu.
- Retro pixel look with Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles (CC0) and pixel fonts.
